# Nordlicht Language School: FAQ (English)

Nordlicht Language School, Beispielstraße 12, 20095 Hamburg. German and English courses for adults since 2014.

## Where is the school and how do I get there? <!-- id: location -->
We are at Beispielstraße 12, 20095 Hamburg, 3rd floor, a 4-minute walk from Hamburg Hauptbahnhof (exit Mönckebergstraße). There is a lift. We have no parking of our own; the nearest public car park is "Saturn-Parkhaus", about 150 m away.

## What are your opening hours? <!-- id: hours -->
The office is open Monday to Friday 9:00–19:00 and Saturday 10:00–14:00. Classes run Monday to Saturday from 8:30 to 21:00. We are closed on public holidays in Hamburg.

## Which languages and levels do you teach? <!-- id: languages-levels -->
We teach German from A1 to C1 and English from A2 to C1 (General and Business English). Every level follows the Common European Framework (CEFR).

## How do I find out my level? <!-- id: placement-test -->
Take our free placement test: a 20-minute online test plus a 10-minute talk with a teacher (on site or by video call). You get your level and a course recommendation the same day. Book it on WhatsApp or at the office.

## How many students are in a class? <!-- id: class-size -->
Group classes have 6 to 12 students. A group starts with at least 5 participants. Private lessons are one-to-one; mini groups have 2–3 people.

## Do you offer online classes? <!-- id: online -->
Yes. Evening courses and private lessons are available online via Zoom with the same teachers and materials. Intensive courses are on site only.

## When do new courses start? <!-- id: start-dates -->
Intensive courses start every 4 weeks; the next start dates are 5 January, 2 February and 2 March 2027. Evening courses start every 10 weeks; the next start is 12 January 2027. Private lessons can start any day.

## Do I get a certificate? <!-- id: certificate -->
Yes. With at least 80 % attendance you get a Nordlicht course certificate with your CEFR level. For official certificates you can take the telc or Goethe exam; we prepare you for both.

## Are you a telc exam centre? <!-- id: exam-centre -->
Yes, we are a licensed telc exam centre. telc German B1, B2 and C1 exams take place at our school once a month. Exam registration closes 4 weeks before the exam date.

## Do you offer the integration course (Integrationskurs)? <!-- id: integration -->
Yes, we are approved by the BAMF for integration courses (600 hours of German plus a 100-hour orientation course). With a BAMF entitlement (Berechtigungsschein) the fee is €2.29 per lesson, and many students are fully exempt. Bring your entitlement letter to the office to register.

## Can my employer pay for the course? <!-- id: invoice-employer -->
Yes. We issue invoices to companies, and many employers pay for job-related courses. We also accept the Bildungsgutschein from the Agentur für Arbeit or the Jobcenter for selected intensive courses.

## How can I pay? <!-- id: payment-methods -->
You can pay by bank transfer, SEPA direct debit, EC card or credit card at the office. Cash is not accepted. Instalments are possible, see the terms and conditions.

## Are course books included? <!-- id: books -->
Course books are not included in the course fee. A course book costs about €30–35 per level and can be bought at the office. Private lessons use free materials from the teacher.

## Can I try a lesson before I book? <!-- id: trial-lesson -->
Yes, one free trial lesson in an evening group, if there is a free seat. Ask us on WhatsApp for the next date.
