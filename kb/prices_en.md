# Nordlicht Language School: Price list 2027

All prices in EUR, VAT-exempt under § 4 Nr. 21 UStG. One lesson = 45 minutes. Prices valid from 1 January 2027.

## German intensive course A1–B2 <!-- id: price-intensive -->
German intensive course, levels A1 to B2: 4 weeks, Monday to Friday 9:00–12:15, 80 lessons, €420 per 4-week module.

## German evening course A1–B2 <!-- id: price-evening -->
German evening course, levels A1 to B2: 10 weeks, twice a week (Tuesday and Thursday 18:30–20:00), 40 lessons, €490 per 10-week course. Also available online via Zoom at the same price.

## German C1 course <!-- id: price-c1 -->
German C1 course: 8 weeks, Monday to Thursday 18:00–20:15, 96 lessons, €690.

## telc exam preparation B1 / B2 / C1 <!-- id: price-exam-prep -->
telc exam preparation course for B1, B2 or C1: 4 weeks, Saturday 9:00–14:00, 24 lessons, €290. The exam fee is separate: telc B1 €170, telc B2 €200, telc C1 €230.

## English courses <!-- id: price-english -->
General English A2–C1 evening course: 10 weeks, once a week, 20 lessons, €280. Business English B1–C1: 10 weeks, once a week, 20 lessons, €340.

## Private lessons <!-- id: price-private -->
Private lessons (German or English), one to one: €55 per 45-minute lesson; 10-lesson package €500. Mini group of 2–3 people: €35 per person per lesson.

## Integration course <!-- id: price-integration -->
Integration course (BAMF): €2.29 per lesson with a BAMF entitlement; free if the BAMF grants a fee exemption. Self-payers without entitlement: €4.58 per lesson.

## Discounts <!-- id: price-discounts -->
Discounts: 10 % for students and trainees with a valid ID; 10 % when you book two modules in a row; 5 % for each friend you refer once they book (max 20 %). Discounts cannot be combined.

## Registration fee <!-- id: price-registration -->
One-time registration fee: €25, waived for the placement test and for integration courses.
